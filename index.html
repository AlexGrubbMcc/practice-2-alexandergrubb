Stagename Djo

Djo is the musical artist alias for well known musician and actor Joe Keery. Originally a disguise so Joe Keery could make sure his music wasn't listened to due to his fame, the project eventually grew into the global phenomenon it is today. His music envelopes the sound of the 70's and 80's, drawing heavily on his time working on the show Stranger Things. The solo project came after his time on the band Post Animals, which he helped start in 2014 and left in 2017. He has since rejoined the band in 2025 and released his last solo album for now that same year.
